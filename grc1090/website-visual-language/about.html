Sunny Side Up first opened its doors in 2008 with one simple dream: to create a cozy neighborhood café where everyone could feel at home. Over the years, our little café continued to bloom, growing into a beloved gathering place filled with smiling families, familiar faces, and happy conversations every morning, rain or shine. What started as a small dream has grown into a community we are so proud to be part of.

At Sunny Side Up, we believe that a great meal starts with great ingredients. That's why we are passionate about supporting our local San Luis Obispo farmers, growers, and artisans. We take pride in sourcing wholesome, organic, and locally grown ingredients whenever possible, bringing the freshness and goodness of the Central Coast straight to your table.

Our menu is thoughtfully created to offer something for everyone, from fluffy pancakes and golden waffles to savory breakfast favorites and delicious brunch creations. We believe that breakfast should be more than just the first meal of the day; it should be a chance to slow down, share a laugh, and enjoy a little sunshine with the people you love. Every plate is made with care, quality ingredients, and a whole lot of heart.

More than anything, Sunny Side Up is about community, connection, and making people feel welcome. We are grateful for every family, friend, and neighbor who has supported us throughout the years. Whether you're a familiar face or visiting us for the very first time, there's always a seat waiting for you at our table. Here's to good food, happy mornings, and a little sunshine every day!
