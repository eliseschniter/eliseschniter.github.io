About us

Sunny Side's Story

Our neighborhood cafe began in 2008 and continued to bloom until it could be found filled with smiling families every morning, rain or shine. Our cafe aims to serve and support locals. All of our ingredients come from local farmers and artisans. We guarantee all of our ingredients to be wholesome and organic in order to sart off your day with the fuel you and your family require.
